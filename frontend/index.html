How it works

Three seconds to a decision

UniTrack answers one question: leave now, or wait? Drivers broadcast their position, dispatchers report the queue, and you get a single number.

1

Track live

Every shuttle on duty shares its GPS position. The map updates continuously, so you see where the bus actually is — not where a printed timetable claims it should be.

2

Check the queue

A shuttle two minutes away is little use if fifty people are waiting ahead of you. Dispatchers publish the queue level, and it feeds directly into your estimate.

3

Decide

We compare riding against walking. When your feet would get you there first, UniTrack says so plainly rather than letting you wait for a bus you don't need.
